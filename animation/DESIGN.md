# DESIGN – Cinematic Dragon Experience

## 1. Design Direction

**Theme:** Dark Cinematic Chinese Dragon

The design must be inspired by the supplied red Chinese dragon reference.

The visual identity should feel powerful, mysterious, elegant and premium.

Avoid generic fantasy-game interfaces.

## 2. Colour Palette

| Element | Colour |
|---|---|
| Main background | #000000 |
| Dragon red | #D00000 |
| Bright red glow | #FF0000 |
| Dark crimson | #690000 |
| Fire orange | #FF6A00 |
| Fire yellow | #FFC247 |
| Primary text | #FFFFFF |
| Secondary text | #A0A0A0 |

## 3. Dragon Placement

The dragon should be positioned prominently without covering essential website content.

### Desktop

- Use a large 3D viewport.
- Allow sufficient space for the long dragon body.
- Keep the camera focused on the head and upper body initially.
- Make the tail visible where screen composition permits.

### Mobile

- Use a closer camera view.
- Reduce unnecessary visual detail.
- Keep navigation and controls accessible.
- Avoid horizontal overflow.

## 4. Cinematic Intro

Sequence:

1. Black screen.
2. Red embers appear.
3. Subtle smoke begins.
4. Dragon gradually becomes visible.
5. Eyes glow.
6. Dragon moves its head.
7. Optional growl plays after user interaction.
8. Experience transitions to idle mode.

Provide ENTER EXPERIENCE and SKIP INTRO controls.

## 5. Idle State

- Continuous natural movement using supported animation.
- Gentle body movement.
- Subtle red eye glow.
- Floating embers.
- Light atmospheric smoke.

Avoid exaggerated continuous motion.

## 6. Roar State

When the user activates roar:

- Dragon performs an appropriate head and mouth animation if supported.
- Red eye glow increases.
- Audio plays.
- A restrained camera impact effect occurs.
- Dragon smoothly returns to idle mode.

## 7. Fire State

When fire is activated:

- Dragon transitions into a suitable pose.
- A fire particle stream appears.
- Fire lighting affects nearby objects.
- Fire sound plays.
- Sparks and smoke gradually disappear.

If no compatible mouth animation exists, use a visual fire effect only after clearly validating its alignment with the dragon.

## 8. Mouse Interaction

- Gentle camera parallax.
- Head tracking where supported.
- Smooth pointer response.
- Limited rotation.
- No unnatural twisting.

## 9. Scroll Interaction

Use selected scroll positions to control:

- Camera movement.
- Dragon position.
- Dragon orientation.
- Atmospheric transitions.

Do not hijack the page's normal scrolling.

## 10. UI Controls

Provide:

- Enter Experience.
- Skip Intro.
- Sound toggle.
- Volume slider.
- Roar button.
- Fire button.
- Replay button.

Keep controls compact and visually consistent.

## 11. Accessibility

- Keyboard accessible controls.
- Visible focus states.
- Accessible labels.
- Reduced-motion support.
- No essential information conveyed only through sound.
- No forced audio autoplay.

## 12. Design Quality Requirements

The result should feel cinematic rather than cluttered.

Use restrained animations, consistent lighting, controlled particle density and readable content.
